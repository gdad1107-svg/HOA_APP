# Glossary

One term, one meaning, used the same way in every requirement, screen, email and export. Terms come from the mock-up (`index.html`). Where the mock-up uses two words for one thing, the **Use** column picks one and the other is listed under **Don't use**.

Revision 1 · Business Analyst · 2026-11-27 · Draft for owner review

## People and places

| Term | Meaning | Don't use |
|---|---|---|
| Household | One lot and the people who live on it. Bills, payments, vehicles and requests belong to a household. | Account (except in "statement of account"), unit |
| Lot | The property, written "Blk 4 Lot 12". A household has one lot until decision D3 says otherwise. | Address, unit |
| Homeowner | A person signed in for a household. | Member (except in legal letters), resident, user |
| Treasurer | The admin who approves payments, issues receipts, sends notices and keeps the books. | HOA admin |
| Admin office staff | Admins who handle households, requests, vehicles, bookings, announcements and record requests. They don't approve payments unless decision D4 says so. | Secretary, office |
| Board member | Reads finances and reports; does not change records. | Director |
| Guard | Verifies guest passes and checks guests in and out at the gate. | Security, guard on duty |
| Guest | A visitor listed on a guest pass. Has no account. | Visitor (except in "Visitor log") |
| Phase | A section of the subdivision used in the board financial report (Phase 1–4). Meaning of "CM" to confirm. | |

## Dues and money

| Term | Meaning | Don't use |
|---|---|---|
| Dues | The monthly amount every household is billed. | Fees (fees are for amenities), assessment |
| Billing run | Creating one month's bills for every household. A month is billed once. | Billing, generating |
| Period | The month a bill is for, written "November 2026". | Month (in tables, use Period) |
| Due date | The last day of the period. | Deadline (a deadline is in a notice) |
| Statement of account | A household's bills, payments and balance. | SOA (in screens), ledger |
| Balance | Amount billed minus approved payments. A negative balance is shown as "credit". | Outstanding |
| Overdue | A bill not fully paid after its due date. | Late, past due (past due is for record requests) |
| Delinquent | A household with at least one overdue bill. | In arrears |
| Good standing | A household with no overdue bills. | Clear, updated |
| Payment | Money a homeowner reports sending. Status: **Pending approval**, **Approved**, **Rejected** or **Void**. | Transaction |
| Reference number | The number on the e-wallet or bank confirmation for a QR Ph payment. | Ref, transaction ID |
| Proof of payment | The screenshot of that confirmation. | Receipt (a receipt is what the HOA issues) |
| Official receipt (OR) | The numbered receipt the HOA issues for an approved payment, e.g. OR-00093. Numbers never skip or repeat. | Receipt (alone), e-receipt |
| Advance payment (credit) | The part of a payment beyond what is billed. | Overpayment (on screens) |
| Void | Cancelling an approved payment or a recorded expense, with a reason. The record stays and is left out of totals. | Delete, reverse |
| Notice | One letter in the collection sequence: **Reminder**, **1st Notice**, **2nd Notice**, **Final Demand**. | Demand letter (except Final Demand) |
| Expense | Money the association spends, with a category and vendor. | Disbursement (except in the board report) |
| Collections | Approved dues payments and amenity fees in a month. Deposits are not collections. | Income, revenue (revenue is a board report term) |
| Fund balance | Opening balance + dues + amenity fees − expenses = closing balance. | Cash, funds |
| Close a month | Lock a month so nothing dated in it can change. | Finalise, lock |

## Amenities, vehicles and requests

| Term | Meaning | Don't use |
|---|---|---|
| Amenity | The swimming pool, function room, pickleball court or basketball court. | Facility |
| Slot | A fixed time range for an amenity, e.g. "07:00–08:00". | Time, schedule |
| Booking | A household's request for one slot. Status: **Pending approval**, **Approved**, **Rejected**, **Cancelled**. | Reservation |
| On hold | What other homeowners see for a slot with a pending booking. | Reserved |
| Fee | The charge for a slot. Counted as income when the booking is approved. | Rate |
| Deposit | A refundable amount held for an amenity. Never counted as income. | Bond |
| Booking pause | The board setting that stops delinquent households from booking. The mock-up calls it both "Block amenity booking" and "Booking is paused"; use **booking pause** in documents and "Booking is paused for your account" on screen. | Block |
| Vehicle registration | A household's request to register a vehicle. Status: **Pending approval**, **Approved**, **Rejected**. | Car registration |
| Sticker | The numbered HOA sticker for an approved vehicle, e.g. #0460. | Pass, decal |
| Request | A maintenance, security, neighbour, garbage or billing issue reported by a homeowner, numbered T-101. Status: **Open**, **In Progress**, **Resolved**. | Ticket, complaint (complaint is a kind of request) |
| Record request | A homeowner asking for a report, certificate or record, numbered RR-101. Status: **Submitted**, **In review**, **Ready**, **Declined**. | Report request (the mock-up uses both) |
| Certificate of good standing | A numbered certificate that a household has no overdue dues, valid 30 days. | HOA clearance (may appear in brackets for recognition) |

## Communication and the gate

| Term | Meaning | Don't use |
|---|---|---|
| Announcement | News posted to all homeowners, shown as a banner until dismissed. | Post, news |
| Important announcement | An announcement shown as a pop-up on sign-in until the homeowner presses "Got it". | Urgent, pinned |
| Notification | A message in the bell for one person. | Alert |
| Guest pass | A homeowner's pass for named guests on given dates, with a QR code and pass code. Status: **Valid**, **Upcoming**, **Expired**, **Cancelled**. | Visitor pass, gate pass |
| Pass code | The 8-character code on a guest pass, written K7QX-M9TA, for when the QR can't be scanned. | PIN, token |
| Scan | Any check of a pass at the gate, by QR or code. Every scan is recorded, including **Invalid** ones. | Check |
| Visit | One guest's arrival and departure. | Entry, log |
| ID photo | The photo of a guest's ID taken at check-in, deleted after the retention period. | ID scan |
